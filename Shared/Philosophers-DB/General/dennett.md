# Dennett, Daniel (1942--2024)

- **Tradition**: Analytic philosophy of mind; naturalism; evolutionary theory; cognitive science
- **Major Works**: *Content and Consciousness* (1969), *The Intentional Stance* (1987), *Consciousness Explained* (1991), *Darwin's Dangerous Idea* (1995), *Intuition Pumps and Other Tools for Thinking* (2013)
- **Key Positions**:
  - The intentional stance: we predict and explain the behavior of systems (people, animals, artifacts) by treating them as rational agents with beliefs and desires, without commitment to the inner reality of those states
  - Multiple drafts model of consciousness: there is no "Cartesian theater" where consciousness happens; consciousness is distributed across parallel, competing narrative streams in the brain
  - Heterophenomenology: the scientific study of consciousness takes subjects' verbal reports as data to be explained, not as authoritative descriptions of inner states
  - The design stance and physical stance complement the intentional stance as alternative explanatory strategies for different purposes
  - Real patterns: beliefs and desires are real patterns in behavior, neither fictions nor mysterious inner entities; they are real but not fundamental
  - Adaptationism about biological design: natural selection is the primary explanatory principle for functional complexity in the biological world
- **Key Arguments**:
  - The argument against the Cartesian theater: there is no single place and time in the brain where "it all comes together"; this undermines both dualist and materialist theater-models of consciousness
  - The argument for the intentional stance: treating systems as intentional yields real predictive power even when we do not know their internal structure; the stance is instrumentally indispensable
  - The argument from intuition pumps: philosophical thought experiments often function as intuition pumps -- persuasive devices that guide thinking in fruitful directions without needing strict validity
- **Relevance**: `phil-charity-check` (the intentional stance as a principle of charity in interpretation), `phil-conceivability-arg` (critiquing intuition-based arguments about consciousness like the zombie argument), `phil-thought-experiment` (analysis of intuition pumps as philosophical tools), `phil-argument-reconstruct` (identifying implicit assumptions in consciousness debates)
- **Related**: Quine (naturalism; Dennett is a self-described Quinean in method and philosophical temperament); Chalmers (principal opponent in consciousness debates; Dennett rejects the "hard problem" as an artifact of confused intuition); Dawkins (meme theory collaborator and evolutionary theorist); Ryle (precursor in rejecting the "ghost in the machine"; Dennett's multiple drafts model is a Rylean descendant)
