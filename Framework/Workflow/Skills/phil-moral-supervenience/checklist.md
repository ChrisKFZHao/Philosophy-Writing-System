# Moral Supervenience Analysis Quality Checklist

> After each moral supervenience analysis, check each item. A fully checked list indicates a high-quality supervenience assessment.

---

## Supervenience Relation Characterization

- [ ] **Strength correctly specified**: Is the supervenience relation correctly classified as weak (within-world) or strong (cross-world), with justification for the classification?
- [ ] **Scope correctly specified**: Is the supervenience relation correctly classified as global (whole-world) or local (individuals/actions), with justification?
- [ ] **Base properties identified**: Is the class of properties constituting the supervenience base (natural, physical, descriptive, etc.) clearly specified?
- [ ] **Supervening properties identified**: Is the class of moral properties claimed to supervene (all moral, thin only, thick only) clearly specified?
- [ ] **Modal force specified**: Is the modal force (conceptual, metaphysical, nomological, or merely de facto) explicitly identified and discussed?

## Blackburn Challenge Assessment

- [ ] **Formulation correctly identified**: Is Blackburn's challenge correctly identified as inference to the best explanation, explanatory burden, McPherson's dilemma, or another formulation?
- [ ] **Challenge reconstructed accurately**: Are the premises of the challenge clearly stated and related to the argument's specific supervenience claim?
- [ ] **Dialectical force assessed**: Is the force of the challenge assessed — decisive objection, significant consideration, or merely dialectical pressure — rather than assumed?
- [ ] **Target specificity noted**: Does the analysis acknowledge that the challenge primarily targets non-naturalist realism and has less force against naturalist reductionism?

## Dreier Triviality Analysis

- [ ] **Triviality challenge engaged**: Does the analysis explicitly address Dreier's objection that supervenience on the class of all properties is trivial?
- [ ] **Non-trivial base specified**: Has a non-trivial, restricted supervenience base been identified, or has the failure to specify one been noted?
- [ ] **Contingency implication assessed**: Has the analysis considered whether restricting the base makes the supervenience claim contingent rather than necessary?
- [ ] **Impact on dialectic evaluated**: Has the analysis assessed whether the triviality challenge merely relocates the debate or undermines it?

## Realist/Expressivist Response Evaluation

- [ ] **Conceptual truth response assessed**: If applicable, has the claim that supervenience is analytic been evaluated with attention to the prospects for conceptual analysis in ethics?
- [ ] **Grounding response assessed**: If applicable, has the claim that grounding explains supervenience been evaluated, including whether grounding preserves non-naturalism?
- [ ] **Reductive identification considered**: Has the reductive identity option been assessed, including its costs (commitment to specific reduction claims)?
- [ ] **Brute necessity option weighed**: Has the possibility of accepting supervenience as brute been fairly weighed against the alternative of accepting other brute necessities?
- [ ] **Expressivist explanation critically examined**: Has the expressivist claim to explain supervenience been assessed for explanatory power, not merely described?
- [ ] **Attitudes supervenience problem addressed**: Has the analysis considered whether the expressivist faces a parallel challenge explaining the supervenience of moral attitudes?
- [ ] **Explanatory burden compared fairly**: Are the explanatory burdens for both realism and expressivism assessed even-handedly, without presuming one side's advantage?

## Cross-Domain Comparison

- [ ] **Relevant domains identified**: If applicable, have relevant comparison domains (aesthetic, mental, semantic) been identified?
- [ ] **Analogies and disanalogies noted**: Have both structural similarities and relevant differences between moral supervenience and supervenience in other domains been discussed?
- [ ] **Parity argument assessed**: If a parity argument is made, has it been evaluated for its dialectical force (does it genuinely weaken the anti-realist challenge)?

## Report Quality

- [ ] **Kim's taxonomy used consistently**: Is the Kim-style specification maintained throughout the report without confusing strength, scope, and modal force?
- [ ] **Dialectical status rating defended**: Is the structured rating (significantly/modesly favors anti-realism/realism, or neutral) supported by the preceding analysis?
- [ ] **Unresolved issues flagged**: Are unresolved tensions or open questions in the supervenience dialectic explicitly noted?
- [ ] **Form of realism specified**: Does the report identify which form of realism (naturalist, non-naturalist, constructivist) is most favored or disfavored by the analysis?
- [ ] **Metaphysical distinctions maintained**: Are key metaphysical distinctions (supervenience vs. grounding, covariance vs. dependence, explanation vs. redescription) properly maintained throughout?
