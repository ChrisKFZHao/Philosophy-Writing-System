# Thought Experiment Analysis Quality Checklist

> After each thought experiment analysis, check each item. A fully checked list indicates a high-quality thought experiment analysis report.

---

## Structural Analysis

- [ ] **Five elements fully decomposed**: Setting, Actors, Events, Key Detail, and Counterfactual Hinge are each separately identified and described — not conflated into a single summary paragraph.
- [ ] **Key Detail distinguished from ornament**: Details that carry argumentative force ("driving details") are explicitly distinguished from those that are merely decorative ("ornamental details"). The basis for this distinction is stated.
- [ ] **Counterfactual Hinge precisely located**: The exact step at which the scenario departs from the actual world is identified, and whether this departure is modest or radical is assessed.
- [ ] **Scenario conceivability assessed**: Whether the scenario is internally coherent and prima facie conceivable is evaluated — not assumed simply because it has been described in words.
- [ ] **Imaginative resistance checked**: Following Gendler (2000), whether the scenario generates imaginative resistance (difficulty "entering" the scenario) is assessed, and whether the resistance stems from logical defect or normative/moral blockage.

## Intuition Mechanism

- [ ] **Intuition source classified**: The source of the intuition is classified as everyday experience generalization, conceptual convention, or moral/aesthetic perception — with justification for the classification.
- [ ] **Intuition reliability assessed**: The reliability of the identified intuition source is evaluated. Everyday generalizations are typically reliable; conceptual conventions risk circularity; moral perceptions exhibit cultural variation — each is assessed accordingly.
- [ ] **Contrary intuition considered**: Whether a reasonable person could have the opposite intuition from the same scenario is actively considered — not assumed away by the analyst's own agreement with the intuition.
- [ ] **Experimental philosophy findings referenced**: Where relevant (especially for moral or folk-psychological intuitions), whether experimental philosophy studies show cross-cultural or demographic variation in the intuition is noted.
- [ ] **Framing effects checked**: Whether the wording or presentation of the scenario might be triggering the intuition through framing rather than through the scenario's content is examined.
- [ ] **No intuition-as-argument conflation**: As the Quality Criteria state, the intuition mechanism is the foundation but not itself an argument — its plausibility is flagged, not treated as self-evident.

## Argumentative Integration

- [ ] **Full derivation pathway mapped**: The chain — scenario description to intuitive response to generalization to conclusion — is explicitly laid out, with each arrow tested for necessity.
- [ ] **Scenario-to-intuition step tested**: Whether the intuitive response necessarily follows from the scenario description, or whether alternative intuitions are equally plausible, is assessed.
- [ ] **Generalization step probed**: The move from "in case C, we have intuition I" to "in all cases of type C, I holds" is tested for representativeness — does a single case license a universal claim?
- [ ] **Hidden universal leap flagged**: If the generalization step smuggles in an unstated universal premise (e.g., "all structurally similar cases elicit the same intuition"), this is explicitly flagged.
- [ ] **Argument role classified**: The thought experiment's role is classified as Core Argument (the conclusion depends on it), Supporting Argument (it reinforces but is not load-bearing), or Heuristic Only (illustrative, not probative).
- [ ] **Default-universality avoided**: As the Quality Criteria state, that an intuition holds in a specific case does not entail it holds in all similar cases — this limit is respected in the generalization assessment.

## Modal Assessment

- [ ] **Modal force classified**: Whether the thought experiment claims logical, metaphysical, physical, or merely conceptual possibility is explicitly identified — not lumped under a generic "possible."
- [ ] **Modal force matches argumentative need**: The modal bar is correctly calibrated: a thought experiment that is merely logically possible cannot refute a claim of metaphysical necessity — the modal strength matches the target.
- [ ] **Counterfactual hinge modal status assessed**: The modal status of the counterfactual departure itself is evaluated — is the hinge epistemically accessible or a radical modal leap?
- [ ] **Actuality-gap calibrated**: The magnitude of departure from the actual world is assessed, and whether intuitions trained on the actual world reliably extend across this gap is questioned.
- [ ] **Sorensen-style logical structure considered**: Following Sorensen (1992), whether the thought experiment's force can be represented as a deductive argument from possible-worlds premises is assessed.

## Template Classification

- [ ] **Thought experiment template recognized**: The structural pattern (e.g., fission case, twin-earth, brain-in-a-vat, trolley variant, zombie argument) is identified from the thought-experiment-templates typology.
- [ ] **Template-argument fit verified**: The identified template is appropriate for the argument the thought experiment serves — a fission template for personal identity, a Gettier template for knowledge claims — misalignment is flagged.
- [ ] **Template expectations checked**: The normative expectations of the template are checked — does the thought experiment meet the structural requirements its template demands, or is it an incomplete instance?
- [ ] **Template limitations acknowledged**: Where the template carries known philosophical baggage (e.g., twin-earth cases presuppose semantic externalism), this dependency is flagged.

## Alternative Scenarios

- [ ] **Alternative reading considered**: At least one alternative interpretation of the scenario — one that would not yield the same intuition or conclusion — is explicitly explored.
- [ ] **De-presuppositionalized case attempted**: For key presuppositions, an alternative case is constructed that removes or varies the presupposition, and the effect on force is tested.
- [ ] **Presupposition necessity determined**: If removing a presupposition collapses the thought experiment's force, that presupposition is flagged as load-bearing; if the experiment survives, the presupposition is flagged as ornamental.
- [ ] **Equivalent ordinary case sought**: Whether the thought experiment's argumentative force can be replicated with a more ordinary, less counterfactual case is explored — if not, the reliance on the exotic scenario is noted.
- [ ] **Counter-thought-experiment considered**: Whether a competing thought experiment exists that drives toward the opposite conclusion is checked, and if so, the dialectical balance is assessed.

## Report Quality

- [ ] **Executive summary present**: A one-paragraph overview covers the thought experiment name, structural pattern, key intuition source, most critical presupposition, and overall force rating.
- [ ] **Scenario decomposition complete**: All five elements (Setting, Actors, Events, Key Detail, Counterfactual Hinge) are present in the output, each with description.
- [ ] **Derivation pathway diagrammed**: The four-step pathway (scenario to intuition to generalization to conclusion) is presented clearly, with each step's vulnerabilities noted.
- [ ] **Force assessment table complete**: All six dimensions (conceivability, intuition reliability, derivational validity, presupposition transparency, contribution to argument) are rated with justifications — no dimension skipped or given a bare label.
- [ ] **Presupposition audit listed**: All background presuppositions are catalogued, each annotated with its impact (Critical, Significant, or Minor) per the Presupposition Transparency Principle.
- [ ] **Source citation complete**: The thought experiment's source (author, text, page/section) is provided, making the analysis auditable.
