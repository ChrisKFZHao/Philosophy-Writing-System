# Intuition Pump Analysis Quality Checklist

> After each intuition pump analysis, check each item. A fully checked list indicates a high-quality analysis.

---

## Pump Mechanism Identification

- [ ] **Mechanism precision**: Is the target cognitive faculty identified with sufficient specificity using the taxonomy (perceptual seeming, conceptual competence, moral sentiment, modal imagination, memory-based recognition, linguistic competence, practical reasoning)?
- [ ] **Primary/secondary distinction**: For hybrid pumps, are primary and secondary mechanisms distinguished, with interactions noted?
- [ ] **Load-bearing details**: Are scenario features that drive the intuition distinguished from incidental narrative details?
- [ ] **Mechanism adequacy**: Does the analysis assess whether the pump actually targets the cognitive faculty the argument requires?

## Intuition Chain Reconstruction

- [ ] **Three-stage reconstruction**: Are the raw intuitive response (Stage A), propositional judgment (Stage B), and argumentative premise (Stage C) each explicitly articulated?
- [ ] **Gap detection**: Where the raw intuition underdetermines the propositional judgment, or the judgment underdetermines the premise, are these gaps flagged and assessed?
- [ ] **Imported content**: Are background assumptions required for the intuition to arise made explicit and checked for question-begging?
- [ ] **Generalization check**: Is the inference from particular judgment to universal premise separately evaluated and licensed?

## Intuition Type Classification

- [ ] **Taxonomy applied**: Is the intuition correctly classified using the principled taxonomy (categorization, moral, modal, conceptual, epistemic, semantic, mathematical, phenomenological, metaphysical)?
- [ ] **Hybrid handling**: Where the pump targets multiple intuition types, are all relevant types noted?
- [ ] **Classification justification**: Is the classification explained, not merely asserted?

## Cognitive Transparency

- [ ] **Transparency level**: Is the pump evaluated as cognitively transparent or opaque, with reasoning provided?
- [ ] **Feeling of rightness (FOR)**: Is the metacognitive experience assessed, distinguishing genuine epistemic confidence from manufactured confidence?
- [ ] **Opacity flagged**: Where the pump is opaque, is the opacity identified as a methodological liability?

## Framing/Bias Detection

- [ ] **Ordering effects**: Would presenting information in a different order alter the intuition?
- [ ] **Affective framing**: Are emotionally charged terms doing argumentative work that the scenario's structure does not?
- [ ] **Implicit contrasts**: Is the reader's judgment anchored by an unstated comparison made explicit?
- [ ] **Selective description**: What is omitted, and would including omitted details alter the intuition?
- [ ] **Rhetorical posture**: Are inevitability phrases ("surely," "obviously," "it is clear that") flagged?
- [ ] **Neutral re-description**: For each detected framing feature, is a neutral re-description proposed and tested?

## Debunking Susceptibility

- [ ] **Three families considered**: Is the intuition evaluated against evolutionary, process, and genealogical debunking?
- [ ] **Premise-conclusion form**: Are debunking arguments stated explicitly and evaluated for soundness?
- [ ] **Outcome classified**: Is each debunking argument classified (fully debunking, partially debunking, consistent with truth-tracking, or inapplicable)?
- [ ] **Debunking restraint**: Has the analysis avoided treating the mere existence of a debunking argument as sufficient to undermine the intuition?

## Dialectical Force

- [ ] **Verdict category assigned**: Is the pump assigned one of the five categories (dialectically decisive, weighty but defeasible, weak, inert, counterproductive)?
- [ ] **Dimensions integrated**: Does the verdict integrate findings from mechanism adequacy, chain integrity, empirical robustness, debunking resistance, and framing independence?
- [ ] **Dialectical alternatives mapped**: Under the counterfactual that the intuition is rejected, is the outcome (collapse, weakening, restructuring, burden-shifting, overdetermination) specified and justified?
- [ ] **Burden calibrated**: Does the analysis calibrate the burden the pump bears against the pump's actual performance?

## Report Quality

- [ ] **Defeasibility acknowledged**: Does the report identify which findings could overturn the verdict if new evidence emerged?
- [ ] **Neutral tone**: Does the analysis maintain methodological neutrality — neither assuming intuitions are worthless nor assuming they are reliable?
- [ ] **Empirical grounding**: Are claims about whether intuitions are "widely shared" supported by citation or explicitly flagged as unverified?
- [ ] **Debunking charity**: Are debunking arguments reconstructed with the same precision and charity as the pump itself?
- [ ] **Dialectical completeness**: Does the report answer "does it matter that this pump fails?" by tracing downstream argumentative consequences?
- [ ] **Boundary cases**: If the target is not a genuine intuition pump (bare intellectual seeming, "intuition" as dismissal label), is this classified and referred appropriately?
