# Conceptual Analysis Template

Template for `phil-conceptual-analysis` output reports.

---

```
===============================================================================
概念分析报告
===============================================================================

日期：{YYYY-MM-DD}
分析对象：{Target concept(s)}
分析者：phil-conceptual-analysis skill v{version}
===============================================================================


0. 分析范围
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

{What concept(s) are being analyzed? In what context? From what text or
theoretical framework? What is the purpose of the analysis?}


1. 候选定义
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 定义 1：{Label}
{Definition statement}

必要条件：
- [N1] {Condition} — {Assessment: holds / problematic / unclear}
- [N2] ...

充分条件：
- [S1] {Condition set} — {Assessment}


2. 案例测试
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

| 案例 | 类型 | 定义1判定 | 定义2判定 | 直觉判定 | 评估 |
|------|------|-----------|-----------|----------|------|
| {Case 1} | 正面 | ✅/❓/❌ | ... | ✅/❌/❓ | {Comment} |
| {Case 2} | 反面 | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| {Case 3} | 边界 | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| {Case 4} | 对比 | ... | ... | ... | ... |

图例：✅ = 符合定义, ❌ = 不符合定义, ❓ = 定义无法判定


3. 区分映射
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 已识别区分

| 区分 | 术语 | 功能 | 是否显式？ |
|------|------|------|-----------|
| {Distinction 1} | {Term A} vs. {Term B} | {What work this distinction does in the argument} | {Yes / No} |
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

### 缺失区分

{Distinctions that are relevant but not drawn in the source text. For each:
what is the distinction, why is it relevant, and what would change if drawn?}


4. 概念结构类型
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

目标概念：{Concept}
结构类型：{classical / prototype / cluster / natural_kind / functional / response_dependent / essentially_contested}

{Justification for the classification. If classical: what are the necessary
and sufficient conditions? If prototype: what are the central exemplars?
If cluster: what are the criteria and how many must be satisfied?}


5. 维度分析
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

| 维度 | 类型 | 范围 | 与其他维度的关系 |
|------|------|------|-----------------|
| {Dimension 1} | {binary / scalar / multi} | {Range description} | {Independent / interacts with D2} |
| ... | ... | ... | ... |


6. 概念网络
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

上级概念：{Superordinate concept(s)}
下级概念：{Subordinate concept(s)}
同位概念：{Coordinate concept(s)}
对立概念：{Opposed concept(s)}
依赖概念：{Concepts this one depends on for intelligibility}


7. 概念工程建议（可选）
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

{If the concept is problematic: refinement, replacement, elimination,
or new introduction options. Each as a suggestion, not an imperative.}


8. 总体评估
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

{1-2 paragraphs: How well-defined is the concept? Does its vagueness or
ambiguity cause problems for the arguments that use it? Is the concept
fit for the theoretical purpose it serves?}

===============================================================================
* 分析完成。概念分析基于文本证据和哲学文献中的标准用法。
===============================================================================
```
