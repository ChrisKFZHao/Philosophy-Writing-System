# Ontological Audit Template

---

===============================================================================

3. 节俭性评估
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 量的节俭性 (Quantitative Parsimony)
{How many entities of each type? Are there unnecessary multiplications?
Ockham's Razor assessment.}

### 质的节俭性 (Qualitative Parsimony)
{How many fundamental categories of being? Could any categories be reduced
to others? Is the category scheme more abundant than needed?}

### 意识形态的节俭性 (Ideological Parsimony)
{How many primitive notions? Are there undefined terms that could be defined
in terms of others?}

### 比较评估
{How does this ontology compare to major competitors in terms of parsimony?
What explanatory work do the additional entities do? Could a more
parsimonious ontology capture the same phenomena?}


4. 争议性承诺
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

{List ontological commitments that are philosophically contested —
entities whose existence is denied by some reasonable philosophical positions.}

| 实体 | 争议 | 接受者 | 否认者 |
|------|------|--------|--------|
| {Entity} | {What is the debate?} | {Who accepts?} | {Who denies?} |
| ... | ... | ... | ... |


5. 量化词分析
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

{Analysis of quantifier phrases in the text and what they commit to:}

- "{Quoted phrase}": {Analysis — does the quantifier carry ontological
  commitment? Can it be paraphrased away? Is the commitment explicit or
  does the author seem unaware of it?}


6. 本体论与认识论区分
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

{Flag instances where the text conflates ontological claims with
epistemological ones:}

- "{Quoted passage}": {Analysis — is this an ontological claim ("X exists")
  or an epistemological claim ("we can know X")? Does the argument slide
  from one to the other?}


7. 总体评估
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

{2-3 paragraphs:
- What is the overall ontological picture presupposed by the argument?
- Is the ontology parsimonious or abundant?
- Are the commitments acknowledged or hidden?
- If the author were forced to explicitly accept or reject each commitment,
  would the argument survive?
- What is the single most controversial ontological commitment, and what
  would happen to the argument if it were rejected?}

===============================================================================
* 审计完成。本体承诺识别基于 Quinean 标准（"to be is to be the value of
  a variable"）和实质本体论（heavyweight ontology）框架。
* 隐式承诺的确定性评估基于文本中量词模式的清晰度。
===============================================================================
